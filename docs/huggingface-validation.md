## Independent review

One read-only independent review found three consequential issues. Each received a failing regression before correction:

1. A second sentence could cancel the first sentence's pending ASR. ASR now has a separate lifetime/queue from coach output; recognized segments are persisted by the server and combined for the next response.
2. Speech-driven dispatch could bypass retry delays. All ASR/turn paths now respect backoff with a bounded queue of at most three segments.
3. Disconnect left an SSE-renewed owner alive. Explicit lease release and stale-lease guards permit another window after disconnect and prevent old release callbacks from clearing a newer connection.

Also reproduced/fixed a silence-only tail after a full long-speech segment. No minor findings were reported. The reviewer explicitly left live model compatibility and physical hardware/echo behavior to live validation; those require user credentials/devices and are not claimed passed.
